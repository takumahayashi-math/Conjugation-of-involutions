The presentation of Lemma 4.10.1 in Yokota's book "Exceptiona Lie groups" is analytic and omits the computational details. Here we give an explicit algebraic derivation. We also discuss its analog for other matrices.

In the sage file, the source is included. We compute the actions as matrices with the standard basis
(xi_1,xi_2,xi_3,c_1,c_2,c_3,eta_1,eta_2,eta_3,d_1,d_2,d_3,xi,eta) of the Freudenthal vector space V(A).
To be precise, we think of c_i, d_i in O as blocks.

Following Yokota's book, we construct the translation matrix delta. We rename it beta to suit the notations to my paper "Some symmetric subgroups of strongly inner exceptional algebraic groups." We check the conjugacy result and compute the attached cocycle.
The results are appended as the result file.
